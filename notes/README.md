# Catalectic — historical notes

Historical reading notes on classical invariant theory, mathematics, verse, scientific imagination, and nineteenth-century terminology. These notes were begun while the repository was named **synectic**.

- [Poetry, science, and mathematics](poetry-science-and-mathematics.md) — Daniel Brown's *The Poetry of Victorian Scientists*; Poe's *The Rationale of Verse*; J. J. Sylvester's *The Laws of Verse* and four-volume *Collected Mathematical Papers*; further literary parallels; source links and research questions.
- [“Synectic” and the Borcherds lecture lead](synectic-and-borcherds.md) — Sylvester's prosodic term; Cauchy/Briot/Bouquet's complex-analysis terminology; unverified recollection of a Richard Borcherds lecture.
- [Sylvester and Synectic in 2025 X screenshots](sylvester-synectic-2025-posts.md) — detailed attribution of the Cauchy–Riemann / Milton–Byron bridge, fluid/continuity metaphors, phonetic syzygy, Poe and the unresolved YouTube comment.

The original bibliographic discussion is at [issue #1](https://github.com/isomorphisms/catalectic/issues/1), but the notes in this directory are the working reference. Direct primary sources and later interpretations are marked separately. An unverified lecture reference is **not** a verified attribution.
